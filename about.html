About the Native Plant Initiative

The Native Plant App is designed to bridge the gap between community conservation and digital accessibility. Our goal is to empower gardeners, students, and land managers with accurate data regarding indigenous flora.

Why Native Plants Matter

Indigenous plants have co-evolved with local wildlife, creating essential interdependencies that non-invasive or exotic ornamentals simply cannot replicate. By cultivating regional species, we sustain critical pollinator populations and improve overall ecosystem resilience.
