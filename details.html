Native Plant Catalog

Discover five essential plants native to Pennsylvania that support local ecosystems and wildlife.

1. Purple Coneflower

Scientific Name: Echinacea purpurea

Description: A hardy perennial featuring bright purple petals and a prominent seed cone that attracts native birds.

2. Butterfly Weed

Scientific Name: Asclepias tuberosa

Description: Famous for its vibrant clusters of bright orange flowers, it serves as a critical host plant for monarch caterpillars.

3. Wild Bergamot

Scientific Name: Monarda fistulosa

Description: Also known as bee balm, this plant produces lovely lavender, tubular flowers loved by pollinators.

4. Black-Eyed Susan

Scientific Name: Rudbeckia hirta

Description: A cheerful yellow wildflower that blooms profusely throughout the summer months across meadows.

5. Switchgrass

Scientific Name: Panicum virgatum

Description: A sturdy native prairie grass that provides exceptional shelter and structural interest in autumn landscapes.
